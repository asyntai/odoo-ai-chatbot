How It Works

  1. Create an account at asyntai.com
  2. Get your Site ID from your Asyntai dashboard
  3. Install this module on your Odoo instance
  4. Click on Asyntai AI Chatbot in the app switcher
  5. Done! The chatbot appears on your website automatically

Configuration

Click on the Asyntai AI Chatbot app in the app switcher to connect your account.

Alternatively, you can get a JavaScript snippet from asyntai.com/dashboard and add it manually.

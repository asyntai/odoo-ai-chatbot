=======================
Asyntai - AI Chatbot
=======================

Create and launch AI assistant/chatbot for your Odoo website in minutes.

Installation
============

1. Download and extract the module to your Odoo addons directory (e.g., ``/addons/asyntai_chatbot/``)
2. Restart Odoo server
3. Go to Apps, click "Update Apps List"
4. Search for "Asyntai" and click Install
5. Navigate to Asyntai AI Chatbot app in the app switcher

Alternatively, if you don't want to use a plugin, you can also get a JavaScript snippet at `asyntai.com/dashboard <https://asyntai.com/dashboard>`_ and manually insert it into your web pages.

Configuration
=============

After installation:

1. Click on **Asyntai AI Chatbot** in the app switcher (or go to Settings > Asyntai AI Chatbot)
2. Click **"Get started"** to connect your Asyntai account
3. Sign in or create a free account
4. The module will automatically save your connection and the chatbot is now live on your site!
5. Set up your chatbot, review chat logs and more at: `asyntai.com/dashboard <https://asyntai.com/dashboard>`_

Don't have an account yet? Create a free Asyntai account at `asyntai.com/auth <https://asyntai.com/auth>`_

Managing Your Chatbot
=====================

Once connected, you can manage your chatbot settings, review chat logs, and customize AI responses at:
`asyntai.com/dashboard <https://asyntai.com/dashboard>`_

Requirements
============

- Odoo 16.0 or higher (tested on 16.0, 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0)
- Website module installed
- Backend admin access for configuration

If your Odoo version is not compatible, you can still use Asyntai by getting a JavaScript snippet from `asyntai.com/dashboard <https://asyntai.com/dashboard>`_
